# Interpreting a report

A match means the supplied strings agree under the documented comparison rule. It does not establish that the person holds a role, that a company owns a name, or that the observation faithfully represents a website.

## Review queue

- **Missing:** collect the field or explain why it is intentionally unavailable. Never convert absence into agreement.
- **Mismatch:** inspect the exact source, observation date and canonical approval. Historical roles, subsidiaries, translations and spelling differences may be legitimate, but the tool cannot decide.
- **Approved alias:** confirm that the explicit alternative is still valid for this source and context. Approval is asserted by the input author.
- **Manual review:** read descriptions for factual scope, dates, attribution and implied relationships. Identical wording can still repeat an error.
- **Match:** retain the source and approval record. Agreement across copied profiles can repeat the same unsupported statement.

## Practical limits

The tool cannot verify source ownership, page existence, historical truth, publisher independence, approval legitimacy, source freshness or whether two profiles describe the same real-world entity. Multiple observations of one source do not imply independent corroboration. There is no evidence credibility score, no crawler, no AI call and no indexing or ranking forecast. A future-date check only checks dates against the supplied as-of date; an incorrectly chosen as-of date can hide collection mistakes.

Keep the owner, approval date and supporting evidence in a separate review log. Avoid committing real personal data or confidential observations. Use separate runs when canonical facts or acceptable aliases vary across markets or periods. Validate all proposed profile edits with the appropriate owner before publishing them.
